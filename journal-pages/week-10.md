## Crit Session 2

This week I presented the latest version of my Emotional Awareness Tool to my tutor and classmates.

The prototype included:

- Daily emotion recording
- Morning, afternoon, and night tracking
- Primary and secondary emotions
- Emotion intensity levels
- Trigger categories
- Reflection notes
- Emotional pattern summaries

At this stage, my goal was to explore how emotional data could help people identify emotional problems and respond to them more effectively.

---

## Feedback from Classmates

After presenting the project, I received feedback from several classmates.

Many people felt that the calendar-based layout was clear and easy to understand. Organising emotions by week, day, and time period helped users quickly recognise patterns and compare emotional changes over time.

Classmates also commented that the p5.js prototype already felt interactive and visually complete. The emotional input system allowed users to actively contribute their own emotional records instead of only viewing information.

Several suggestions were also provided for future development.

One suggestion was to explore emotional data from multiple users rather than focusing only on personal records. This could allow comparisons between different people and create a broader understanding of emotional patterns.

Another suggestion was to make the long-term purpose of the project clearer. While the emotional recording system worked well, the overall impact of the collected data could be communicated more effectively.

Some classmates also suggested increasing interaction and audience engagement through additional features and comparisons.

---

## Reflection on Feedback

The feedback session encouraged me to think more carefully about the purpose of the project.

Originally, I described the project as a tool that could help people solve emotional problems. However, after discussing the project with classmates and my tutor, I realised that the visualisation itself cannot directly solve emotional issues.

The system can reveal emotional patterns, behaviours, and habits, but it cannot provide treatment or direct solutions.

This made me reconsider what the project is actually trying to achieve.

---

## Updated Project Direction

After reflecting on the feedback and discussions from the critique session, I decided to change the focus of the project.

### Previous Direction

Help people solve emotional problems.

### Updated Direction

Help people better understand their emotions through personal data.

The revised project focuses on emotional awareness rather than emotional intervention.

The purpose of the project is now to help users:

- Recognise emotional patterns over time.
- Identify recurring emotional triggers.
- Understand when specific emotions occur most often.
- Reflect on their emotional habits and behaviours.

This new direction feels more appropriate for a data visualisation project because it focuses on making emotional information visible rather than attempting to provide solutions.

---

## Reflection

This week became an important turning point in the project.

The feedback from classmates and my tutor helped me realise that emotional awareness is a more realistic and achievable goal than trying to solve emotional problems through data visualisation alone.

I learned that data visualisation can be valuable even when it does not provide answers. Instead, it can help people discover patterns and insights that were previously hidden.

The most important lesson from this week was understanding the difference between helping people solve a problem and helping people understand a problem.

For the next stage, I will continue improving the emotional awareness system and explore ways to make emotional patterns more visible, meaningful, and easier to interpret.
